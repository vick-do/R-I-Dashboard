What every one of them obeys
—One y-axis. Two measures of different scale get two charts, never a second scale.
—Colour follows the entity, not its rank. Filtering never repaints the survivors.
—Nominal categories get one hue for every bar; length already carries the value.
—Past seven series, fold the tail into "Other" or facet. Never an eighth generated hue.
—Selective direct labels — the endpoint, the extreme — never a number on every point.
—A table view on every chart, and hover that enhances rather than gates.
